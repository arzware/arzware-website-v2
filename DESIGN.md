# Arzware V2 — The Business Evidence Board

## Core idea

Most agencies begin with services. Arzware begins with evidence: the unanswered message, the repeated task, the spreadsheet nobody trusts, the opportunity without an owner. The website behaves like a living case file. Fragments of business friction are pinned, traced, sorted, and turned into one useful next move.

## Visual language

- Warm field-paper base, carbon ink, cobalt blue, and signal orange.
- Oversized editorial typography paired with monospaced case-file annotations.
- Torn notes, stamps, route lines, registration marks, underlines, and physical depth.
- Alternating paper and near-black chapters; no glassmorphism or generic SaaS cards.
- Arzware’s original mark remains the identity anchor.

## Signature motion

1. The hero evidence board responds to the pointer as notes drift at different depths.
2. A red thread continuously traces from scattered symptoms toward one useful move.
3. Scroll reveals behave like documents being filed, stamped, and pulled across a worktable.
4. The method appears as a route rather than four identical cards.
5. Motion is simplified on mobile and disabled for reduced-motion users.

## Narrative

1. Bring us the messy part.
2. Read the evidence before proposing technology.
3. Diagnose → Connect → Build → Improve.
4. Show solution patterns grounded in actual business symptoms.
5. Explain accountable connections and supervised youth experience.
6. Invite one Business Improvement Review.

## Originality guard

This direction preserves Arzware’s editorial seriousness and business-first positioning from the original website, but replaces its particle sphere / living-system metaphor with a tactile evidence-board and field-file system. It must not regress into gradient blobs, feature-card grids, dashboard mockups, or generic AI visuals.
